Arduino-Based Mini Control Panel — Personal Build

Showcase project · designed and wired by me

A working miniature control panel in a real industrial enclosure, built to practice panel craft — and control logic — on my own time. The hardware side is a proper panel build: 24VDC power distribution from a Mean Well EDR-120-24 supply on DIN rail, terminal blocks, a plug-in relay, slotted wire duct, and a panel-mounted green pilot light.

The Arduino Uno runs a program I wrote: a two-road traffic signal controller — a proper state machine. It cycles Road A green → "PREPARE TO STOP / YELLOW FLASHING" → an all-stop "CLEARING ROAD" safety phase → Road B green, driving two banks of red/yellow/green LEDs and a buzzer, with the current state shown live on the LCD. Same sequencing discipline as an industrial start/stop routine, at bench scale.
